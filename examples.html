Dugout Data: what the reports look like

Each team upload makes three kinds of report. Pick one below to see a full example. These use a made-up player and team, not real kids.

For parents & guardians · one per player

A detailed look at one player's season: what each stat means in plain English, how the player compares with the team (without naming any teammates), whether they're improving, and the one thing most worth working on.

Open full screen ↗
For the player · one per player

A shorter, encouraging version written straight to the kid. It celebrates what's going well and gives one simple goal, with no comparisons to teammates.

Open full screen ↗
For coaches & staff only · one per team

The whole roster on one page: each player's strength and focus, practice groups, team trends, and the full stat table. It shows every player's name, so it's for staff, not for sharing with families.
