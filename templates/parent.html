What the Numbers Mean

A stat built from only a handful of at-bats can look great or terrible just from luck. A kid who gets 2 hits in his first 4 at-bats of the season isn't necessarily "a .500 hitter" — that's only 4 tries, and it's just as likely to have been a lucky couple of swings as a sign of real skill. The more at-bats a number is built from, the more it reflects how he actually hits, rather than one hot or cold stretch. So every number below is tagged with how much to trust it yet:
